# Product image requirements

No competitor image has been downloaded or reused. Draft products deliberately contain no media URLs.

For each approved design, prepare:

1. Square or 4:5 front-facing product cutout on warm ivory, minimum 1800 px on the long side.
2. Styled room mockup with truthful scale and a clearly identified product size.
3. Close material/finish detail showing edge and layer construction accurately.
4. Side or shadow view showing actual depth after thickness is confirmed.
5. Installation/mounting visual matched to the supplied hardware and wall type.
6. Packaging image after drop/protection testing.

Export WebP or AVIF where WordPress/browser support is confirmed, preserve a high-quality source file, strip unnecessary metadata, and write specific alt text that describes the product—not keywords. Arabic artwork must be manually approved before it appears in any image.
